How it works

  • Each challenge hides a flag that looks like CTF{something}. Find it, submit it, bank the points.
  • Hints are available but cost points. Progress saves in your browser.
  • Work in a team, talk to each other, and use the Toolkit tab and web search for learning.
Rules of engagement: everything here is fictional or simulated. In the real world, only test systems you own or have written permission to test. In the UK, unauthorised access is a crime under the Computer Misuse Act 1990.

Where next?

  • TryHackMe: guided, browser-based hacking labs with beginner learning paths (some content is free).
  • picoCTF: free CTF platform and practice gym aimed at students.
  • OverTheWire: free wargames that teach Linux and security skills step by step.
  • NCSC CyberFirst: UK government programme with courses and opportunities for young people.
